# Jaemin Cosmology One-Page Summary

## Status

Korean working-paper manuscript. This is a speculative formal framework, not an empirically confirmed scientific theory.

## Core Hypothesis

The brain may be modeled as an interface between higher-dimensional informational constraint structures and low-dimensional conscious experience. The framework treats consciousness, intuition, cognitive efficiency, AGI efficiency gaps, and subjective time as linked modeling targets within one formal hypothesis space.

## Mathematical Skeleton

- `X(T) in M^n`: an information state evolving in an effective higher-dimensional information manifold
- `C = P(X)`: conscious experience as a low-dimensional projection
- `S = Q_c(N_b, F)`: subconscious representation combining neural state and informational constraint structure
- `cal(I) = G(S)`: intuition as a compressed signal derived from subconscious representation
- `dτ/dT = α(X,T)`: internal or subjective time rate, when the subjective-time model is used

## What the Paper Claims

- Conscious experience can be represented as a limited projection of a richer information state.
- Intuition can be modeled as compressed and lossy access to latent structure.
- Human and AI intelligence differences may be framed as an efficiency gap.
- Subjective time can be modeled through an internal-time-rate function.
- The framework can generate candidate predictions for later testing.

## What the Paper Does Not Claim

- It does not claim empirical confirmation.
- It does not claim that the brain literally accesses extra physical dimensions.
- It does not replace standard physics, neuroscience, cognitive science, or AI research.
- It does not treat intuition as supernatural.
- It does not argue that AGI is impossible in principle.

## Candidate Predictions

- Efficiency-gap prediction: comparable generalization and adaptation may require very different energy or compute budgets across humans, animals, and AI systems.
- Intuition-task prediction: fast decisions that are difficult to consciously explain may correlate with high-dimensional neural latent structure, compression, or information-bottleneck measures.
- Subjective-time prediction: after accounting for memory density, novelty, routine, and attention, residual subjective-time effects may be modeled as a candidate `λ` term.

## Why This Matters for AGI

The framework reframes AGI as an efficiency-gap problem rather than an impossibility claim. It asks whether human cognition achieves broad adaptive competence under severe energy, data, and hardware constraints in a way that current AI systems do not yet match.

## Current Limitations

The framework is not empirically validated. Core parameters such as `c`, `η`, `λ`, and `α` require operational definitions. Candidate predictions must be compared against standard neuroscience, cognitive science, psychology, physics, and AI models.
