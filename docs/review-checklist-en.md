# Review Checklist

Use this checklist to evaluate whether the manuscript keeps its intended scope and review-draft status.

## Status and Release Framing

- [ ] Korean `v0.1.0-ko` is treated as the DOI-bearing release.
- [ ] DOI is stated as https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20506269.
- [ ] English Draft v0.2.0 is not treated as a separate DOI release.
- [ ] English Draft v0.2.0 is clearly treated as a review draft.
- [ ] Bibliography verification remains pending.

## Claim Strength

- [ ] Claim strength is cautious.
- [ ] No empirical confirmation is claimed.
- [ ] No verified theory language is used.
- [ ] Candidate predictions are not presented as confirmed.
- [ ] Modeling implications are not presented as empirical laws.
- [ ] Measurement proxies are not presented as final measurements.

## Scope Boundaries

- [ ] No literal extra-dimensional access is claimed.
- [ ] No replacement of physics, neuroscience, cognitive science, psychology, or AI research is claimed.
- [ ] No AGI impossibility claim is made.
- [ ] No AGI guarantee claim is made.
- [ ] No physical causality violation is implied.
- [ ] Intuition is not treated as supernatural.
- [ ] Subjective-time acceleration is not treated as a proven single-cause phenomenon.
- [ ] Life-expectancy and civilizational-acceleration material is framed as speculative heuristic extension.
- [ ] Rotational mechanisms are optional special cases, not core assumptions.

## Mathematical and Operational Clarity

- [ ] `P`, `Q_c`, `G`, and `Q_F` are defined consistently.
- [ ] `c`, `η`, `α`, and `λ` are treated as formal parameters requiring operationalization.
- [ ] `Q_F` is described as an alignment/coupling component, not external injection.
- [ ] `η_proxy` is treated as a candidate operational proxy.
- [ ] `D_felt(a,T)` is clear as modeled felt duration.
- [ ] Physical time is clearly separated from internal or subjective time.

## Baseline Models and Testability

- [ ] Ordinary latent-variable models are acknowledged.
- [ ] Predictive-processing models are acknowledged.
- [ ] Representation-learning models are acknowledged.
- [ ] Information-theoretic models are acknowledged.
- [ ] Cognitive-efficiency models are acknowledged.
- [ ] Baseline comparisons are requested before higher-dimensional interpretations.
- [ ] Candidate predictions identify measurable variables or clearly say operationalization is needed.
- [ ] Potential falsifying or weakening observations are identified.

## Literature and References

- [ ] Missing literature is flagged separately from metadata verification.
- [ ] Weak or unsupported claims are marked for citation review.
- [ ] No unverified DOI, ISBN, arXiv, publisher, journal, page, or URL metadata is introduced.
- [ ] Bibliography citation placement remains a pending follow-up.
