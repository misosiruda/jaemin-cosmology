## Project Summary

The manuscript proposes a speculative formal framework in which the brain may be modeled as an interface between higher-dimensional informational constraint structures and low-dimensional conscious experience. It organizes questions about conscious experience, subconscious representation, intuition, cognitive efficiency, the AGI efficiency gap, and subjective or internal time within a shared mathematical vocabulary.

The framework is not presented as an empirically confirmed scientific theory. It does not claim that the brain literally accesses extra physical dimensions, and it does not claim to replace standard physics, neuroscience, cognitive science, psychology, or AI research.

## Review Request

Please provide critical review focused on weakness detection, not endorsement. The author is not asking reviewers to validate the framework as true.

Useful review areas include:

- Conceptual clarity
- Mathematical consistency
- Terminology and notation clarity
- Claim-strength control
- Falsifiability and candidate predictions
- Relation to ordinary latent-variable, predictive-processing, representation-learning, information-theoretic, and cognitive-efficiency models
- AGI efficiency-gap framing
- Subjective-time modeling
- Identifiability problems
- Missing or weak literature support

## Important Caveats

- Candidate predictions are not confirmed predictions.
- `η_proxy` and related quantities are candidate operational proxies, not final measurements.
- The AGI discussion is an efficiency-gap lens, not an impossibility claim.
- Subjective-time modeling is not a claim that physical time accelerates with age.
- Life-expectancy and civilizational-acceleration material should be read as speculative heuristic extension.
- Rotational mechanisms are optional special cases, not core assumptions.
