## Distribution Notes

- Start with 3-5 technical sanity-check reviewers.
- Do not present the review request as a request for endorsement.
- Do not describe the English draft as a separately released or DOI-bearing version.
- Do not imply empirical confirmation.
- Do not imply literal extra physical dimensions.
- Do not claim replacement of standard physics, neuroscience, cognitive science, psychology, or AI research.
- Do not frame the AGI section as AGI impossibility or AGI guarantee.
- Do not treat reviewer literature suggestions as verified bibliography metadata until independently checked.
