# Scope and Non-Goals

This document clarifies the public scope and non-goals of the `Jaemin Cosmology` manuscript.

## Current Status

The manuscript is a Korean working paper. It proposes a speculative formal framework for organizing consciousness, intuition, cognitive efficiency, AGI efficiency gaps, and subjective time within a shared mathematical hypothesis space.

This manuscript is not an empirically confirmed theory of physics, neuroscience, or consciousness.

## Scope

The manuscript explores:

- conscious experience as a low-dimensional projection
- subconscious or non-conscious processing as a high-dimensional representation
- intuition as compressed access to latent structure
- human intelligence and AGI as an efficiency-gap problem rather than an impossibility claim
- subjective time as an internal-time-rate modeling problem

## Non-Goals

The manuscript does not claim to replace standard physics or neuroscience.

It does not claim that the brain literally accesses extra physical dimensions.

It does not treat intuition as a supernatural ability.

It does not claim that AGI is impossible in principle.

## Research Value

The purpose of the framework is to define a structured hypothesis space and candidate predictions.

For the framework to become empirically meaningful, it must eventually produce measurable predictions that can be distinguished from standard models in neuroscience, cognitive science, physics, and AI.
