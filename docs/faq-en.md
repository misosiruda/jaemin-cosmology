# FAQ

## 1. Are you claiming that the brain literally accesses extra physical dimensions?

No. The current framing is about higher-dimensional informational constraint structures. It does not claim that the brain literally reaches into hidden physical dimensions.

## 2. Is this a theory of physics?

No. It uses mathematical language that may resemble physics or geometry, but it is not a verified physical theory.

## 3. Is this empirically verified?

No. The manuscript is a speculative formal framework and working paper. Its predictions are candidate predictions, not confirmed results.

## 4. Is this compatible with standard neuroscience?

It is intended to be compared with standard neuroscience, not to replace it. If the framework cannot produce predictions distinguishable from standard neural computation models, it may remain only an interpretive reformulation.

## 5. How is this different from ordinary latent-variable models?

The framework connects latent representation, conscious projection, intuition, cognitive efficiency, AGI efficiency gaps, and subjective time under one formal vocabulary. This broader unification is the proposal, but it still needs operational definitions and empirical constraints.

## 6. What would count as evidence against the framework?

Evidence against it would include failure to define measurable parameters, inability to distinguish its predictions from standard models, or empirical results showing that its proposed residual effects disappear under better-controlled neuroscience, psychology, or AI models.

## 7. Why connect this to AGI?

The AGI connection concerns computational efficiency. The framework asks whether human cognition achieves broad adaptive competence under energy, data, and hardware constraints that differ sharply from current AI systems.

## 8. Does the framework imply that AGI is impossible?

No. It does not argue that AGI is impossible in principle. It frames the issue as an efficiency gap, not a proof of impossibility.

## 9. Is intuition treated as supernatural?

No. Intuition is modeled as a compressed and lossy signal derived from subconscious or latent representation. It is not treated as prophecy or supernatural knowledge.

## 10. What is the current status of the manuscript?

The current release track is a Korean working-paper manuscript. It is not a final peer-reviewed article and is not an empirically confirmed theory.
